ROLLER FIELD GUIDE

Before you roll

Moving around. Use A/D or the arrow keys to roll left and right. Use Space, Up, or W to jump. Hold Shift to dash forward and become briefly untouchable.

Getting past danger. Jump over spikes and lava. Cracked blocks crumble after you stand on them, and moving platforms can carry you.

Fighting enemies. Stomp enemies from above or shoot them with a collected gun. Aim with the mouse and click, or tap the playfield in mobile mode. Ammo runs out.

Weapons. You can find sidearms, shotguns, lasers, grenade launchers, homing guns, burst rifles, and boomerang guns.

Health. Hits cost health. Press R to restart after you die.

Combos. Defeat enemies quickly to build combos and trigger brief slow motion.

Shards and the shop. Collect cyan shards, then visit the mechanic near the finish after the enemies are defeated to buy upgrades.

The Fate Wheel. Press G once per level for a random buff or debuff.

Winning. Defeat every enemy, then reach the flag.

Other keys. P pauses, R restarts, E starts endless mode, and the fullscreen button enlarges the game.

Credits. Music and sound effects are generated by the game's built-in AudioFX system.
