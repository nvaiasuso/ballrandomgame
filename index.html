ROLLER FIELD GUIDE

Before you roll

Moving around. Use A/D or the arrow keys to roll left and right. Use Space, Up, or W to jump. Hold Shift to dash forward and become briefly untouchable.

Getting past danger. Jump over spikes and lava. Cracked blocks crumble after you stand on them, and moving platforms can carry you.

Fighting enemies. Stomp enemies from above or shoot them with a collected gun. Aim with the mouse and click, or tap the playfield in mobile mode. Ammo runs out.

Weapons. Drops include shotguns, wide-spread Scatterguns, fast Miniguns, lasers, grenade launchers, homing guns, burst rifles, boomerangs, Piercers that punch through targets, and Ricochet guns that bounce off walls.

Health. Hits cost health. Press R to restart after you die.

Combos. Defeat enemies quickly to build combos and trigger brief slow motion.

Shards and the shop. Collect cyan shards, then visit the mechanic near the finish after the enemies are defeated to buy upgrades.

The Fate Wheel. Press G once per level for a random buff or debuff.

Winning. Defeat every enemy, then reach the flag.

Other keys. SHIFT+E starts Endless; SHIFT+O toggles the secret-ring photo; Y wipes shot splats. P pauses, R restarts, and the fullscreen button enlarges the game.

Credits. Music and sound effects are generated by the game's built-in AudioFX system.

PRE-ENTRY UPGRADES

BEFORE YOU PLAY

ROLLER is meant to be hard.

Expect to get hit. Expect to die. That is part of this game: watch the enemies, learn their tells, and try again.

The enemies adapt and gang up

They can dodge your shots, spot or hear you, call nearby backup, flank while others suppress you, and change their aim based on how you move, jump, and dash. Some take cover, leap over hazards, flee when hurt, charge or slam, pick up weapons and shields, or recover health.

Enemy roster: Hunters, Brutes, Sprinters, Turrets, Hoppers, Rippers, Tanks, Snipers, Swarms, Wardens, Flyers, Chargers, Splitters, Blinkers, Burrowers, Trappers, Burst Gunners, Laser Wardens, Shield Guards, Medics, Bombers, and Gliders. Their attacks include aimed shots, shot patterns, homing or bouncing bullets, lasers, mines, explosions, teleports, burrowing, and splitting into extra enemies.

Bosses escalate: they summon reinforcements, change phases, teleport, and use shockwaves, floor spikes, aimed bursts, lasers, and bullet-hell spreads. Ambushes, a pursuing lava wall, and occasional chaos events can make a run even rougher.

Quick controls: LEFT/RIGHT or A/D move; SPACE/UP/W jumps; SHIFT dashes; SHIFT+E starts Endless; aim with the mouse and click or press X to shoot. Defeat every enemy, then reach the flag.
